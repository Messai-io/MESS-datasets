## Abstract

Research data documenting operation of a bioelectrochemically-improved anaerobic
digester (AD-BES) processing genuine fishery processing wastewater. Supports
publication in Journal of Water Process Engineering (DOI:
10.1016/j.jwpe.2024.105848).
