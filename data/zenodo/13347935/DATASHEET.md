## Abstract (from Zenodo)

<p>The provided repository contains the data, processing/calculations, and plots of the manuscript 'Boosting the Oxygen Evolution Reaction Performance of Ni-Fe-Electrodes by Tailored Conditioning' (DOI: <a href="https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/celc.202400318" target="_blank" rel="noopener">10.1002/celc.202400318</a>). In our manuscript, we introduced electrochemical conditioning of Ni&minus;Fe-based electrodes as a promising electrode design method for the alkaline oxygen evolution. Conditioning was optimized regarding scan rate, potential limits, hold times, and treatment time. More details can be read in the manuscript. The (processed) data is available as .dta-, .csv, and .txt-files. Additionally, the .opju- and .xlsx-files are given, which show the data processing.</p>
