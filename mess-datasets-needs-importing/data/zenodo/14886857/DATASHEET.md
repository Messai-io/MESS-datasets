## Abstract (from Zenodo)

<p>The uploaded data files contain <strong>Gas Chromatography-Flame Ionization Detector (GC-FID) with Autosampler (ALS)</strong> analysis results for liquid samples obtained from electrochemical decarboxylation experiments. These experiments were conducted as part of <strong>Work Package 2 (WP2) of the EBIO project</strong>, focusing on electrochemical transformations of carboxylated compounds.</p>
<p>The GC-FID technique was employed to analyze the composition of reaction products, identifying and quantifying key volatile organic compounds formed during the electrochemical decarboxylation process. The use of an <strong>autosampler (ALS)</strong> ensured consistent and automated sample injection, improving reproducibility and efficiency in data collection.</p>
