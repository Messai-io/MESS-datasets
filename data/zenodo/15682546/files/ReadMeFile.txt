______About the dataset______
NiCoP catalyst is prepared by electrochemical deposition on the Ni substrate. The effect of the deposition solution composition is investigated
and thorough optimisation of the prepared catalyst performed. The properties of the of the synthetized material are investigated by SEM-EDS, TEM, XPS,
BET, linear sweep voltammetry and electrochemical impedance spectroscopy. The technique of the electrochemical doposition is used to prepare catalyst
layer on the Ni foam substrate. Performance of the prepared electrode is verfied under the conditions of the alkaline water electrolysis.
Results show that optimized NiCoP catalyst can achived Platinum like activity for hydrogen evolution reaction under alkaline conditions. 

______Methods of data collection______
* Morphology, elemental composition and physico-chemical properties
	HW: Scanning electron microscope Hitachi S4700, Energy dispersive X-ray spectroscopy, Transmition electron microscope JEM-2100F, X-ray fluorescence spectrometer Axios,
	X-ray photoelectron spectroscope SPECS Phoibos 150 hemispherical analyser, Quantachrome Instruments for BET analysis 

* Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy
     HW: Autolab M204
     conditions: 0.1 or 1 M KOH; 50 °C; 30 mV amplitude; frequency range from 10 kHz to 0.1 Hz; 1.8 V cell voltage

* Linear sweep voltammetry: 
     HW: Autolab M204
     conditions: 0.1 M KOH; 25 °C; RHE; 0 – (-10) mA cm^2

* Alkaline water electrolysis
     HW: Autolab M204
     conditions: 0.1 M or 1 M KOH; 50 °C; 1.5 – 2.0 V

* Alkaline water electrolysis stability test
     HW: Autolab M204
     conditions: 1 M KOH; 50 °C; 400 mA cm^-2

______File name structure_____
* VZx_000_111_XXXXX_Y_0000_v1
     VZx – research proposal (3 characters)
     000 – order of the publication in the research proposal (3 char.)
     111 – last 3 numerals of the superior indicator (3 char.)
     XXXXX – institution abbreviation (max. 5 char.)
          VSCHT - University of chemistry and technology, Prague
          CVUT - Czech Technical University
          UFCH - Institute of Physical Chemistry, Czech Academy of Sciences
          UK - Charles University
          VUT - Brno University of Technology
     Y – file type (1 char.):
          M – manuscript
          D – data
          S – supplementary information
          P – published version of the arctile
     0000 – order of the files in the dataset (4 char.)
     v1 – versioning (v + version number)

* example: VZ1_001_023_VSCHT_D_0001_v1.csv
