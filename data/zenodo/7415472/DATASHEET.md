## Abstract (from Zenodo)

<p>Within the frame of the ELECTRA No. 826244 H2020 project, a microbial electrochemical snorkel (MES) was applied for the treatment of soil contaminated by hydrocarbons at a laboratory scale. The changes in soil ecotoxicity were followed by plant tests applying <em>Sinapis alba</em> and <em>Triticum aestivum</em>. The datasets (supplementary to Figure 7) for this publication are presented here. Referred publication:&nbsp;Carolina Cruz Viggi, Matteo Tucci, Marco Resitano, Bruna Matturro, Simona Crognale, Vikt&oacute;ria Feigl, M&oacute;nika Moln&aacute;r, Simona Rossetti, Federico Aulenta,&nbsp;Passive electrobioremediation approaches for enhancing hydrocarbons biodegradation in contaminated soils,&nbsp;Science of The Total Environment,&nbsp;845,&nbsp;157325,&nbsp;2022, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2022.157325.</p>
