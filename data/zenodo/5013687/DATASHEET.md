## Abstract

Data supporting research on electron uptake mechanisms in Shewanella oneidensis.
Genome-wide gene disruption screen of ~99% of non-essential genes; identified
five genes indispensable for electron uptake from a cathode.

## Files

- 8 xlsx files corresponding to main Figure 3 + Supplementary Figures S1-S7
- Mutant performance data (chronoamperometry, CV)
- Strain-level current measurements (cathodic + anodic, μA)
- Identifies SO_0181, SO_0400, SO_0841 and two complement strains as
  electron-uptake-essential genes
