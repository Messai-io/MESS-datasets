Files: 

1. The file “1.DF_continuous_pH7.xlsx” contains data from the Dark Fermentation (DF) experiments’ performances (continuous fermentation reactor operated at pH 7 and with foodwaste as substrate)

	1.1. The file “1.DF_continuous_pH7_1.csv” or the tab with same name in “1.DF_continuous_pH7.xlsx” file, shows the main parameters analyzed of the Dark Fermentation (DF) experiments’ performances:
		- Rows from 1 to 4 show:
		- Columns B to N: the stoichiometry conversion to transform g/L to g COD/L
		- Columns Z to AL: The initial g of VS, TS and the corresponding g of COD and gCOD/L at each stage 
		- Rows from 5 to 41 show the results of the main parameters assessed in the reactor along the operation. Results are divided into 3 different stages (3 different OLRs: 30, 45, and 60 g VS/Ld)
			Herein can be found: Biogas composition (%), VFAs concentrations in g/L and g COD/L, and other calculations such as COD-VFAs/CODin, tCOD and sCOD of the effluent, COD 	removal, or efficiencies in COD or VS/TS basis as well as the VFAs profile 
			Cells whose days are highlighted in purple were the ones from which microbial populations were analyzed
		- Rows from 44 show the average values and standard deviations of the parameters at the stationary phase of each stage (30,45 and 60 g VS/L*d)

	1.2 The file “1.DF_continuous_pH7_2.csv” or the tab with same name in “1.DF_continuous_pH7.xlsx” file shows the VFAs profiles obtained based on the data from the first tab.
	1.3 The file “1.DF_continuous_pH7_3.csv” or the tab with same name in “1.DF_continuous_pH7.xlsx” file shows the VFAs production obtained based on the data from the first tab along the whole experiment.
	1.4 The file “1.DF_continuous_pH7_4.png” or the tab with same name in “1.DF_continuous_pH7.xlsx” file shows the figure of biogas composition obtained based on the data from the first tab along the whole experiment.

   
2. The files “2.MECs_R1R2_InocMix_Feed_DF_ORL60_pH7.xlsx” contains the performance results during the operation of the two Microbial electrochemical reactors R1 and R2, fed with DF effluent obtained during the operation at an organic loading rate of 60 g VS/Ld.

	2.1 The file “2.MECs_R1R2_InocMix_Feed_DF_ORL60_pH7_1.csv” or tab "R1" from "2.MECs_R1R2_InocMix_Feed_DF_ORL60_pH7.xlsx" file contains results of measured metabolites by HPLC, in g/L; mM; and COD/L.  		Also the recorded H2 volumes and the theoretical ones based on the accumulated charge during the batch. The calculations of MEC performance i.e. coulombic efficiency (CE); cathodic efficiency 		(rCAT); and global yield (rH2), are also included in these tabs.
	2.2 The file “2.MECs_R1R2_InocMix_Feed_DF_ORL60_pH7_2.csv” or tab "R2" from "2.MECs_R1R2_InocMix_Feed_DF_ORL60_pH7.xlsx" file contains results of measured metabolites by HPLC, in g/L; mM; and COD/L.  		Also the recorded H2 volumes and the theoretical ones based on the accumulated charge during the batch. The calculations of MEC performance i.e. coulombic efficiency (CE); cathodic 				efficiency (rCAT); and global yield (rH2), are also included in these tabs.
	2.3 The file “2.MECs_R1R2_InocMix_Feed_DF_ORL60_pH7_3.csv” or tab "chronoamperometries" from "2.MECs_R1R2_InocMix_Feed_DF_ORL60_pH7.xlsx" file, contains current densities recorded with the software EC-		Lab® d during batch assay for R1 and R2 with an applied potential of 256 mV vs Ag/AgCl using a potentiostat Biologics VMP3. Also calculations of coulombs, accumulated charge and 				electron equivalents are contained in this tab in columns J to R.
	2.4 The file “2.MECs_R1R2_InocMix_Feed_DF_ORL60_pH7_4.csv” or tab “cyclic voltammetry” from "2.MECs_R1R2_InocMix_Feed_DF_ORL60_pH7.xlsx" file, contains the current intensities for the two MECs R1 and R2 		at the beginning of the batch operation recorded during a scan at different applied potentials from -0.6 to 0.6 V vs Ag/AgCl at a scan rate of 1mV/s. 


3. The file “3.Enrichment.xlsx” contains all electrochemical data retrieved during the enrichment of two bioanodes (BA1 and BA2) in a monochamber reactor operated as n-stat with an applied potential of 256 mV vs Ag/AgCl. The medium used for these experiments was synthetic medium with acetate as substrate. These enriched bioanodes where then used in separated double-chamber electrolysis reactors R1 and R2 (file series 2).

	3.1 The file “3.Enrichment_1.csv” or the tab “chronoamperometries" in “3.Enrichment.xlsx” file, contains current densities recorded with the software EC-Lab® with an applied potential of 256 mV vs 			Ag/AgCl using a potentiostat Biologics VMP3.
	3.2 The file “3.Enrichment_2.csv” or the tab “cyclic voltammetries” in “3.Enrichment.xlsx” file, contains the cyclic voltammetries of the bioanodes at the begging of the two enrichment cycles, 			confirming electro biofilm development in the bioanodes. 


4. The file “4.OTUS N-Stat and MECs_R1R2.xlsx” contains all the OTUS detected and the relative abundance in the MEC reactors (enrichment and R1 R2 reactors. 
	
	4.1 The file “4.OTUS N-Stat and MECs_R1R2_1.csv” or the tab “ENRICHEMENT” in “4.OTUS N-Stat and MECs_R1R2.xlsx” file, reports the OTUS during the enrichment in the bulk of the n-stat reactor and in the 		electrodes surface (BA1 and BA2). 
	4.2 The file “4.OTUS N-Stat and MECs_R1R2_2.csv” or the tab “R1R2” in “4.OTUS N-Stat and MECs_R1R2.xlsx” file, reports OTUs and relative abundance by the end of the batch operation of MEC reactors R1 and 		R1 fed with DF effluentin the anodes and in the bulk phase.


5. The file “5.OTUs Continuous DF experiments.xlsx” or the file “5.OTUs Continuous DF experiments.csv”, contains the OTUs detected and their absolute abundance. 

All the sequences of OTUs detected during dark fermentation experiments and MEC assays, both enrichment and experiments with DF effluent were deposited in the NCBI database under the bioproject name PRJNA1152534.
