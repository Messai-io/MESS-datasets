## Abstract

Research data on recovering ammonium and phosphate through a three-chambered
microbial electrolysis cell system, with the objective of producing struvite as
a recoverable product from livestock manure.

## Files

- `Dataset struvite GEMBESH.xlsx` — 3 sheets:
  - `Reactor_data` — pH, ammonium, phosphate, struvite formation
  - `Potentiostat_data` — current, voltage
  - `Minteq_Results` — mineral speciation (Struvite, Brucite, Hydroxyapatite,
    Ca3(PO4)2)
